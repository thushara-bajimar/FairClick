The quiet revival of sourdough bakeries

By M. Duarte

Across small towns, neighbourhood bakeries are reopening with long-fermented loaves and shorter menus. Bakers say customers want slower food and fewer additives.

The trend began during the pandemic, when home baking surged, and has outlasted it as a new generation of bakers opened shops of their own.
